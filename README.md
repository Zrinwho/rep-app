# REP

一个训练记录。像一杯冰水：不甜，不吵，刚好需要。

A training log. Like a glass of ice water: plain, quiet, just what is needed.

---

## 中文

### 为什么

练力量需要的东西其实不多：今天练什么，上次做到哪，这次要不要加重，休息够了没有。

REP 只做这几件事。没有动态，没有排行，不推送，不打扰。打开，记下，放下。

### 它做什么

- **今天练什么**：按你的计划排好当天的动作。
- **加不加重**：上次每组都达到目标次数，这次加一档；没达到，就保持。
- **组间休息**：做完一组自动倒计时。锁屏或切去听歌时也会准时提醒，不打断音乐。
- **一周练了多少**：每块肌肉一周的组数，用肌群图看。直接发力记 1 组，辅助发力记半组。
- **进步**：每个动作的预估 1RM 曲线，每次训练后的一页小结。
- **成就**：七个等级，从白到蓝宝石，门槛参考常见的力量标准。
- **导入**：训练计划的截图、照片或 Excel；体测报告的照片或 PDF。中文、英文、德文都能识别。

### 它不做什么

- 不需要账号。
- 没有服务器，数据只在你的手机里。可以导出备份，也可以导入。
- 没有广告，没有统计，不收集任何东西。

### 下载

在 [Releases](https://github.com/Zrinwho/rep-app/releases/latest) 下载最新的 `REP-x.x.apk`，在手机上打开安装。第一次安装时，系统会询问是否允许安装来自浏览器或文件管理的应用，选允许即可。

之后的新版本可以在 App 里直接更新：设置 → 应用更新。

- 安卓 5.1 及以上
- 中文 / English

## English

### Why

Strength training needs little: what to train today, where you got to last time, whether to add weight, whether the rest was long enough.

REP does only that. No feed, no ranking, no pushes. Open it, log, put it away.

### What it does

- **Today**: the lifts of the day, from your plan.
- **Add weight or not**: every set reached its target last time, so one step up this time; otherwise stay.
- **Rest**: a countdown starts after each set and alerts on time with the screen locked or in another app, without stopping your music.
- **The week**: sets per muscle over the last seven days, on a muscle map. Direct work counts as one set, assisting work as half.
- **Progress**: an estimated 1RM curve for every lift, and a one-page summary after each workout.
- **Awards**: seven ranks, from white to sapphire, set by common strength standards.
- **Import**: a plan from a screenshot, photo or Excel file; a body-composition report from a photo or PDF. Chinese, English and German.

### What it does not do

- No account.
- No server: the data stays on the phone. Backups can be exported and imported.
- No ads, no analytics, nothing collected.

### Download

Get the latest `REP-x.x.apk` from [Releases](https://github.com/Zrinwho/rep-app/releases/latest) and open it on the phone. The first time, Android asks whether to allow installs from the browser or file manager: allow it.

Later versions update inside the app: Settings → App updates.

- Android 5.1 or later
- 中文 / English
